01 · The Stack, In Plain Terms

Three systems. Zero servers to babysit.

The site is not "hosted on a server" the old way. It is built from three modern pieces that work together — each one removing a job a developer used to have to do by hand.

The Engine
Astro
Builds every page in advance

Instead of assembling each page live when a visitor lands (the old, slower way), Astro pre-builds the entire site ahead of time. Benefit: pages open almost instantly anywhere in the world, and there's no live database sitting behind the site waiting to be overloaded, hacked, or breached.

The Editor
TinaCMS
A visual editor wired to the real content

You edit text and images the way you'd edit a shared document — click, change, see it, save. Benefit: no ticket to a developer, no waiting for "the next release" to fix a typo or swap a photo.

The Delivery
Vercel
Publishes automatically, worldwide

The moment content is saved, the platform rebuilds and redistributes the site across a global network. Benefit: every change goes live in about a minute, with no downtime and no separate hosting bill to negotiate or manage. It's the same hosting platform already running the club's Prismic-based site today — no new vendor, nothing new to learn.

Every content change becomes live in seconds — no developers on call, no downtime, no server maintenance bills.

02 · Under the Hood

This isn't a budget website. It's engineered.

Five architectural choices that quietly do the work of an entire technical team — turned into what they actually mean for the brand.

01 Pre-rendered pages, no live database per visit

Among the fastest load times technically possible on the web today. Visitors — and Google's ranking algorithm — both reward speed with attention.

02 Every piece of content has a typed schema

It is structurally impossible to publish an event with no date, or a page with no SEO title. Quality is enforced by the system, not left to whoever is typing that day.

03 Every edit is a Git commit

A complete history of every version of the site, with instant rollback to any past state. There is no "we lost the homepage and nobody remembers what it looked like."

04 Native SEO & AI-readiness

Structured Schema.org data, an automatic sitemap, and meta tags on every page — built to be read correctly by Google and by AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini, not only by a human clicking around.

05 Bilingual IT / EN, routed automatically

One platform serves both languages cleanly, without duplicating the site or doubling the effort of keeping it up to date.

06 Every piece of content is editable from the CMS — forms included

Add a new event or news item and update the copy on its sign-up form in the very same place. One free tool now replaces two separate systems — the old CMS and Jotform — for simpler operations and lower cost.

Quality, history, findability and cost
aren't a promise here —
they're enforced by the architecture.

05 · Built for Growth

Marketing intelligence, not just a website.

Speed and structure aren't the only technical edge. The site also tracks where visitors really come from, and the forms adapt to who's filling them out.

01 UTM parameters carried page to page

The tags that identify a campaign, an ad or a channel are captured on arrival and stay attached for the entire visit — all the way through to the form. Real ROI per channel, not attribution guesswork.

02 Conditional logic, checked against Perfect Gym in real time

The main Contact form checks the email against the Perfect Gym member database as it's typed, then adapts on the spot:

  • Existing member → routed straight to a service request
  • New prospect, activity already chosen → sent to the right team for it
  • General enquiry → default routing to the front desk

One form, several journeys — automatically, with no extra staff time.

A graphometric signature that auto-generates a signed PDF contract, Stripe built in, and a direct connection to Perfect Gym — so enrolling, paying and activating membership happen in one motion.

Every submission also plants a VID

A VID is a Visitor ID — a small, anonymous marker assigned the first time someone lands on the site. It doesn't identify a person by itself, but it lets the site recognise the same visitor across different visits. Every form submission is tied to that VID, and every time that visitor comes back to the site — even without filling anything in — a quiet "ping" is logged against it.

Not yet connected to the CRM — but built to be. Once it is, this single signal can power:

  • Automatic lead scoring, based on how many times a visitor returns
  • An instant back-office alert to recontact a hot lead right away
  • Automated communication flows — email, SMS or WhatsApp — triggered by that behaviour

10 · What This Site Achieves

Five goals. One strategic asset.

01 Speed

Google measures load time as a ranking factor, and most visitors abandon a page that takes more than a few seconds to appear. A slow site quietly loses both search position and visitors, every day. This one is built to be among the fastest technically possible.

02 Ease of Use

A site that needs a developer for every small change is a cost. A site the team can update itself, in minutes, is operational value — time and money that stay with the club, not an agency retainer.

03 Scalability

New sections, new languages, new features — added on top of what already exists, not instead of it. Built to absorb the club's next few years of change, not just today's.

04 Stability

No live database to breach, no third-party plugin to break on the next update, every version kept and recoverable. Pre-rendered, versioned code is about as close to unbreakable as a website gets.

05 Independence

No complex CMS, no intermediary standing between the club and its own site. No vendor lock-in, no licence to renew, zero management cost beyond the hosting it already runs on.

A website as a method, not a showcase.

A strategic, operational asset that runs the whole customer journey — from the first click, to the active member.
