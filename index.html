01 · Purpose of the Project

Following the corporate separation from Aspria Holdings, Tennis Club Ambrosiano must become fully independent — in its systems, its data, its operations and its administration. This project manages that transition end to end: carving out every shared platform, rebuilding what TCA needs to own directly, and doing it silently, so that members never notice the change. The club's history, identity and premium positioning stay completely intact; the infrastructure underneath becomes its own.

02 · Objectives

01
Seamless Separation

Carve out every system shared with Aspria — member platform, website, CRM, Microsoft tenant, devices — with zero downtime and zero impact on members.

02
Independent Infrastructure

Stand up TCA's own digital stack, built in parallel and switched on only when 100% ready: surgical, invisible, fully owned by the club.

03
Compliance & Continuity

Fiscal, accounting, membership and data obligations covered from day one — including the migration of the accounting software by the December deadline.

04
Full Visibility

Every workstream tracked and every decision documented on this site, so the whole team always knows where the project stands.

04 · How We Work

This site is the single home of the project: one place for all files, all shared reasoning and all meeting minutes. No scattered attachments, no out-of-date copies — what you see here is always the current state of the work.

  1. Always Up to Date

    The progress of every workstream is tracked here as the work advances — starting with the Carve-Out Roadmap, where each scope shows its owner, status, next step and dates.

  2. Everything Published Here

    All content — analyses, deep-dives, technical documents — is shared directly on this site and added to the menu the moment it is ready. If it matters to the project, it has a page here.

  3. Shared Reasoning, Not Just Conclusions

    Each analysis includes the sources, the comparisons and the rationale behind the recommendation, so every decision can be reviewed and challenged at any time by anyone on the team.

  4. Meeting Minutes On Record

    The minutes of every project meeting are archived here as well — decisions, action items and owners — so nothing depends on memory or buried email threads.

  5. One Single Source of Truth

    One place for all the files, all the shared thinking and the full history of the project. New members of the team can get up to speed by reading this site from top to bottom.
