Option A
Register under TCA
Not recommended
+ Simple setup today
− Migration required later — time, technical risk, potential downtime
− Every digital asset tied to the club's legal identity and governance
− Limits future optionality: services resale, external partnerships
− Digital and operational risk not separated from the club
Option B
Independent Entity
Recommended
+ Clean separation from day one — no future migration needed
+ Club delegates digital ops to a focused, lean structure
+ Tech, digital marketing, and accounting under one governance model
+ Future optionality: resell services, attract partners, isolate risk
+ Lean digital model built to scale beyond a single club
What this
entity covers

Technology infrastructure (all accounts above) · Digital marketing & advertising (Meta BM, analytics, campaigns) · Accounting & reporting — from a tech governance perspective, with operational scope to be confirmed · Future commercial services (resell, partnerships, white-label) if the model evolves. The club retains its own legal identity and operations; this entity manages the digital layer on its behalf.
