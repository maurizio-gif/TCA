The Carve-Out Plan

One goal: zero interruptions.

Operations keep running without disruption — every function previously managed by the group is re-allocated in-house, technology by technology.

01 · Perfect Gym
Contract Under TCA

Move the contract directly to TCA, stripping out everything superfluous from the current agreement — and optimise the platform from day one to cover more functions, such as newsletters.

02 · Website
Prismic to TCA — or a New Site

Evaluate transferring the current Prismic site under TCA — carefully assessing the entire tracking and form system currently running on HubSpot. Alternatively, the opportunity to build a brand-new TCA website from scratch.

03 · Forms
Clean Break from HubSpot

Remove the HubSpot account. New HTML forms integrated directly with Perfect Gym: a clean, rebuilt data flow — detached from HubSpot, at minimal cost.

04 · Email · Outlook 365
Account Ownership

Assess whether account ownership can be transferred to TCA; alternatively, plan the migration of accounts to a new Outlook 365 or Google Workspace tenant.

05 · Newsletter
HubSpot → Perfect Gym

Move newsletters out of HubSpot into Perfect Gym — all member communication consolidated in the platform the club already owns.
