Block 1 · sales cycle

Fewer systems on the sales cycle

Products and receipts

Proposed

  • Tilby retired: 800 €/year for 5-6 products
  • PerfectGym sells the products, the front desk already knows how
  • Only the 22% VAT transactions (non-institutional) are fiscalised
  • Pay-per-use fiscal system, to be chosen

Playtomic and Stripe

Source to choose

  • Playtomic through its API, or confirmed payments straight from Stripe
  • Manual transactions are already entered on Stripe
  • Revenue split by area and deferrals handled on the accrual side
  • To check: where Stripe shows the transactions

SAT and juniors

Next step, not now

  • Today: forms and Excel, then loaded by hand into PerfectGym
  • PerfectGym is used for a fraction of what it offers
  • Later: one platform next to PerfectGym for the SAT, about a month of work
  • Now: the day-to-day operation stays as it is, to avoid retraining

In this phase the operation changes as little as possible; the system takes the data from where it already is.

Block 2 · assistants

Assistants that read, propose and explain

Receipts and expenses

Photo in, fields out: vendor, date, total, VAT, category. Duplicates and unreadable photos are flagged.

Supplier invoices

The XML is read by code. The assistant proposes department and account, and flags a price jump or a duplicate.

Reconciliation

Rules match most movements. The assistant proposes the rest and says why: “QUOTA OTTOBRE ROSSI” becomes member Rossi, October charge.

Monthly commentary

A first draft of the variance comments for the management view, edited by a person before it goes anywhere.

The assistant proposes, the code checks, a person confirms.

The system runs with the assistants switched off.

Minimum data, nothing retained, tested on reference documents.

People and software

Who keeps the books, and what I build

A bookkeeper is needed

Person

  • Someone must verify, post and close: the system prepares, it does not decide
  • Studio Foresta, or an internal or freelance accountant, to be named
  • Reviews the exception list and the monthly package; keeps VAT, F24 and the statements

Accounting software with an API

Tool, to select

  • Today CODA, which stops on 31 December
  • The system posts entries and reads balances through the API, no more file hand-offs
  • Short list: TeamSystem Contabilità in Cloud first, Odoo as alternative
  • To agree with the Studio, which works in DATEV

What I build and run

Developer

  • Data in from every source, with the reconciliations and the bank transfer matching
  • The operator workspace and the Studio package
  • The management dashboard with the KPIs
  • Connection to the accounting software; handover to TCA

Three pieces that depend on each other: the accountant decides, the software records, the system prepares.
